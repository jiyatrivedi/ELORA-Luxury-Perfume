Haute Parfumerie

The Olfactory Architecture

The tripartite evolution of fine perfumery: unfolding across hours from top to base notes.

Phase I • 0 to 30 mins

The Head (Top Notes)

Volatile citrus zest, pink pepper, and sparkling bergamot greet the senses — the initial, electrifying introduction that leaves the first impression.

Phase II • 30 mins to 4 hrs

The Heart (Corpo)

Bulgarian rose absolute, Grasse jasmine, and precious saffron form the emotional core, radiating warmth and intimate luxury on warm skin.

Phase III • 4 to 24+ hrs

The Base (Sillage)

Hand-aged Cambodian oud, bourbon vanilla, and Mysore sandalwood provide monumental longevity — the ghost note that lingers on silk pillows.

Our Heritage

Created to leave
an eternal impression.

ELORA was born from a singular conviction: that true luxury is felt, not seen. Founded in a sun-drenched atelier in Grasse, France, our master perfumers spend years sourcing ingredients at the edge of possibility — wild Cambodian oud aged in dark oak, Bulgarian rose absolute harvested at dawn, and Madagascar bourbon vanilla.

Every bottle that leaves our studio is more than fragrance. It is a statement of presence — an invisible garment worn closest to the skin. We craft in micro-batches, never compromising, never rushing. Because a signature, once earned, lasts a lifetime.
